Membership

Choir fee

$35

One fee for every choir. It covers the choir shirt, music, and the year’s events. Pay in Presto with join code faubionchoir.

Forms and fees were due August 28. If you still need to finish, email Ms. Pineau — the directors will work it out.

New 6th graders

Beginning Choir is the sixth-grade ensemble. Students wear the choir shirt, blue jeans, and closed-toe shoes for concerts.

Questions about joining next year? Write Ms. Pineau or Ms. Kroesch.

Optional, and worth it

Private voice lessons

Students meet with Mr. Loeffler once a week during choir for 22 minutes. Lessons are $25 and open to every singer, from beginners to students preparing for UIL, All-Region, and Solo & Ensemble.
