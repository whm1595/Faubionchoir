About us

The directors

Call the school at (469) 302-6900, ext. 42717, or email either director. Private lessons are taught by Mr. Loeffler.

Head choir director

Bridgette Pineau

Ms. Pineau has taught middle school and high school choir for the past 8 years. She graduated from the University of North Texas with a Bachelor of Music in Vocal Music Education, magna cum laude, in 2019. Before McKinney ISD she taught in Carrollton-Farmers Branch ISD and Lewisville ISD.

She is alto section leader at Spring Valley UMC and a member of TCDA and TMEA. Outside school she likes working out, reading, and really bad reality TV.

bpineau@mckinneyisd.net

Associate choir director

Laurel Kroesch

Ms. Kroesch is beginning her 6th year of teaching. She graduated from the University of North Texas with a Bachelor of Music in Vocal Music Education in 2021, with honors. Before McKinney ISD she was head director of a 6th–12th grade program and, most recently, an assistant choir director in Lewisville ISD.

She is a member of TCDA and TMEA. She likes Jersey Mike’s, dance class, and walks with her dog, Penny.

lkroesch@mckinneyisd.net
