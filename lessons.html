During the school day

Private voice lessons

Lessons are offered through the choir program and open to every choir student. They are optional, and the directors encourage them.

The teacher

Michael Loeffler

Mr. Loeffler is a music educator entering his seventh year of teaching. He grew up in Washington state, started choir in fifth grade, and earned a bachelor’s degree in music education from John Brown University in Siloam Springs, Arkansas. He has taught choir and theater in Colorado and Texas.

He also teaches elementary music and middle and high school theater part-time at Grace Covenant Academy in Frisco. His voice students have done well at UIL, All-State and All-Region auditions, and Solo & Ensemble. He likes board games, and time with his wife Emily and their cat, Muffin.

michaelthemusicteacher@gmail.com

How it works

$25 · 22 minutes

Students meet with Mr. Loeffler once a week during their choir class. He is also available at lunch. He chooses repertoire in several styles that fits each singer, from beginners to students who want the next step.

Think of it as a personal trainer for the voice. Lessons are not required to be in choir.
