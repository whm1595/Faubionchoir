Fees, forms, and how to help.

One parent or guardian needs a Presto account. Extra adults can have access too — email Ms. Pineau. Students without internet get paper notes to take home.

Concert clothes

6th grade

Choir shirts go home in September, pop-show shirts in May. Wear the shirt, blue jeans to the ankle (no capris or shorts), and closed-toe shoes.

7th and 8th grade

Fall concert and pop show: the same shirt, jeans, and closed-toe shoes. Formal uniforms are issued from October through March.

  • Treble singers wear a choir dress and their own closed-toe black dress shoes.
  • Tenor and bass singers receive a black shirt and blue tie. They bring black dress slacks, a black belt, black socks, and black dress shoes. This is a change from previous years. Email Ms. Pineau if slacks are a problem.

Do not alter a uniform or use hemming tape. Dresses are altered by the program’s seamstress, Jenny Law. Do not wear cologne or body spray with the uniform. If a piece is lost or damaged, replacement prices are dress $62, slacks $17, shirt $14, and tie $12.

What counts as a grade

Choir is a performance class. Concerts and competitions are required, usually three or four a year, and they are major grades. Daily grades cover punctuality, the choir rules, participation, attitude, teamwork, and rehearsal etiquette.

If a student must miss a concert, give Ms. Pineau written notice at least 48 hours ahead. Family emergencies and severe illness are excused, and the student gets an alternate assignment. Students who do not participate appropriately in class may be kept from the performance and given that assignment instead.

Extra rehearsals before concerts are required. Families get notice in advance. Bella Voce and Tenor Bass are not required to do Solo & Ensemble or All-Region this year. Both are still encouraged.

Volunteer

Parents help throughout the year. McKinney ISD requires a volunteer application and background check every school year. Processing can take up to two weeks. Approved volunteers show a photo ID at the campus. The district keeps application information only to decide whether someone may volunteer.
